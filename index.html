// About

I've spent years in SIEM and SOC engineering — watching organisations struggle with Essential Eight compliance. The same story everywhere: manual patching, manual access reviews, manual backup checks, manual evidence gathering. Most companies either can't staff it or hire expensive consultants who leave them a PDF and a gap.

I started building agents to do this work autonomously. Scan for vulnerabilities. Enforce MFA. Test backups. Generate evidence. 24/7, on-prem, open source. The kind of tooling I wished existed when I was on the other side of the table.

I'm based on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland. I publish my work openly because I think the community gets stronger when people share what they build — not just what they sell.

This site is Richard's personal platform. The work is done through Dobson Development Pty Ltd.
