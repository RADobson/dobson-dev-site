Services / Claude setup & engineering

Make Claude useful in your working day.

Dobson Development helps Australian businesses introduce Claude to their teams, support everyday use and commission custom engineering when the work calls for it.

Discuss your setup

Begin with a real job

Choose a task your staff already do: preparing a meeting brief, summarising material they are authorised to use, drafting a customer response or reviewing a document. We help define a useful result, the source information Claude needs and the review a person must perform.

That gives the rollout a practical purpose and a clear way to judge whether it saves time or improves the work.

Setup and adoption

We help plan the appropriate business account, invite staff, agree access and introduce the relevant features. Any connections to business information should use the permissions needed for the task. Staff need to know which information they may use, who can access it and when a person must check the result.

Initial configuration, rollout and training are scoped with your implementation. Your business contracts with and pays Anthropic directly for its Claude subscription.

What everyday support means

  • Help with access, invitations and using the agreed Claude environment.
  • Questions about existing features, projects and approved connections.
  • Troubleshooting a problem in the supported configuration.
  • Practical guidance on using existing tools and checking their outputs.
  • Helping identify when a problem needs to be raised with the vendor.

Everyday Claude user support forms part of the A$99 per person per month managed support service, plus GST. Software subscriptions are additional.

When the work becomes engineering

A custom integration, an automated business process or software that calls an API needs design, permissions, testing and maintenance. We scope that engineering separately, with the required behaviour, boundaries and acceptance criteria agreed for the project.

For example, helping someone use an existing connection is support. Building a new workflow that reads an enquiry, drafts a response and updates a customer system is engineering. API usage and third-party service charges are separate from the support fee.

Keep people in control

AI output needs review, especially before it becomes advice, a customer commitment or a change to business records. We help define which actions require approval, what happens when information is missing and how a failed workflow is handled. A useful deployment makes those boundaries clear to the people using it.

Questions before you start

Do you resell Claude subscriptions?

No. Your business contracts with and pays Anthropic directly. Dobson Development provides setup, user support and separately scoped engineering services.

Does the monthly support fee include custom automation?

No. Custom Claude integrations, workflow automation and engineering are separately scoped and priced. The monthly fee covers management and everyday support for the agreed environment.
