Services / Google Workspace migration

Move to Google Workspace with a plan.

Keep your business email address and move to tools your team knows. Dobson Development helps Australian businesses plan and carry out a migration from Microsoft 365 to Google Workspace.

Discuss your setup

Start with what your business actually uses

A migration involves more than copying a mailbox. We map users, shared addresses, calendars, files, permissions and the applications that connect to your current environment. That includes the spreadsheets, shared folders and business processes people rely on every day.

Your plan identifies the data to move, the material to retain, the work that needs testing and any Microsoft functionality that still has a job to do.

A migration with clear stages

  1. Discover: review accounts, mailboxes, file locations, sharing and application dependencies.
  2. Prepare: configure the agreed Google Workspace accounts, domain settings and access controls.
  3. Pilot: test a representative mailbox, files and everyday tasks with a small group.
  4. Move: transfer the agreed data and coordinate the email cutover around your business.
  5. Check: confirm mail delivery, calendar access, file permissions and staff sign-in.
  6. Settle in: help staff use Gmail, Drive, Docs, Calendar, Meet and Chat in their daily work.

Treat specialist workflows individually

Excel macros, SharePoint workflows, shared mailboxes, retention requirements and third-party integrations each need their own assessment. We record what will transfer directly, what needs redesign and what may need a different approach. A pilot gives your team a chance to check the result before the wider move.

Migration and ongoing support

Questions before you start

Can we keep our email addresses?

Yes, when you control the domain. The migration changes the service behind your business email address. Domain settings and mail delivery need to be planned and checked as part of the move.

Will all Microsoft files and workflows work identically?

Not necessarily. Complex documents, macros, permissions and integrations need assessment and testing. Your move plan should identify exceptions before the migration.
