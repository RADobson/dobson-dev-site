About Dobson Development

Better tools.
A business behind them.

Dobson Development is a Sunshine Coast technology business helping Australian teams work with Mac, Google Workspace, 1Password and Claude.

Meet Richard Dobson

Richard founded Dobson Development after serving in the Royal Australian Navy and working in cybersecurity across technology vendors and Australian managed services. He holds a Bachelor of Information Technology and a Graduate Diploma in Information Technology.

That background shapes the service: understand the business, check the technology, make access and responsibilities clear, and give people a practical way to get help.

Watch Richard introduce the business in his 2026 SAAB Australia Rising Star Award pitch.

Change your Outlook on IT.

We help businesses consider a workplace built around the tools their people want to use. Mac provides the computer, Google Workspace handles email and collaboration, 1Password manages shared credentials, and Claude supports everyday knowledge work.

Every recommendation starts with the applications your business relies on. We assess the fit and plan the transition before committing to a new setup.

Clear commercial boundaries

Management and support is A$99 per person per month plus GST. All software subscriptions are billed separately. Clients contract with and pay Anthropic directly for Claude. Migration, rollout and custom Claude engineering are scoped separately.

We explain the proposed environment, responsibilities and support arrangements in your quote and service agreement.
