Services / Mac deployment & management

Every Mac ready for the person using it.

A business Mac should arrive with a clear setup process and keep working as the team grows. Dobson Development plans Mac deployments and ongoing device management for Australian businesses.

Discuss your setup

Choose the build around the role

We start with the work each person needs to do: their applications, files, screens, peripherals and remote-working requirements. We assess existing Mac computers as well as proposed purchases, and check essential applications before standardising a configuration.

The result is an agreed build that can be repeated for new staff, with a record of the settings and software the business depends on.

From enrolment to handover

  • Review device ownership, Apple business enrolment eligibility and the management approach.
  • Configure the agreed applications, updates and security settings.
  • Set up work accounts and the access each role needs.
  • Check encryption, recovery arrangements and device inventory.
  • Test key applications, printing and everyday workflows.
  • Give staff a practical handover and a clear route to support.

Management continues after setup

Device management supports repeatable configuration, software deployment and visibility of your Mac computers. We use the agreed management platform to maintain the supported build and help resolve problems. The platform and licence costs are listed separately in your proposal.

Joining and leaving processes connect device work with Google Workspace and 1Password account changes. Recovery and offboarding steps should be tested before they are needed.

A considered move from Windows

Switching computers also changes habits. We identify familiar shortcuts and show staff how to find files, use their applications and ask for help. Windows-only software, specialist hardware and existing integrations are checked during planning rather than assumed to work.

Separate the equipment, licences and service

Hardware, software subscriptions and deployment project work are scoped separately. Management and support for the agreed workplace costs A$99 per person per month plus GST. Device-management, Google Workspace and 1Password licences remain additional; Claude is contracted directly with Anthropic.

Questions before you start

Do we have to buy new Mac computers?

Not automatically. We first assess the Macs you already own, their supportability and the applications your staff need. The proposal identifies any equipment that needs replacing.

Which device-management platform will you use?

We confirm the management platform and buying arrangement in your proposal, based on the supported build and your requirements. Its subscription is separate from the management fee.
