Three house rules

You own it

Every tool that goes in has to let you take your data and your automations with you when you leave. If it locks the door behind you, it stays out.

It works on Fridays

Your email, your chat, your files and your security come from different places, so one bad morning at one vendor leaves the rest of the business standing.

It answers to you

Anything sensitive can run on hardware in your own building, under Australian law, with your name on the asset register.
