One place to ask for help

A new starter needs a Mac. Someone cannot get into their email. A shared password needs to reach the right people. Your team wants help using Claude. We look after the day-to-day technology across Mac, Google Workspace, 1Password and Claude.

The service starts with understanding your applications, people and current setup. That gives us a shared view of what we manage, how your staff ask for help and which work needs a separate project.

What we manage

  • Macs: setup, device management, updates and everyday troubleshooting.
  • Google Workspace: staff accounts, email, calendars, files, sharing and access questions.
  • 1Password: business vaults, shared access and help using the password manager.
  • Claude: everyday user support and help getting started with the agreed tools.
  • Staff changes: a repeatable process for joining, changing roles and leaving.

Check the fit before making the move

We review the applications your business depends on, including specialist software, spreadsheets, printers and customer workflows. If an application needs Windows, we identify that requirement before proposing the rollout. The move plan sets out what can move, what needs testing and what should stay in place.

How the monthly fee works

A$99 per person per month, plus GST, is the management and support fee. All software subscriptions are billed separately. Google Workspace, 1Password and device-management licences are separate line items. Your business contracts with and pays Anthropic directly for Claude.

Custom Claude integrations, automations and engineering are quoted separately. Migration and onboarding requirements are scoped with your move plan. Support hours, response targets and the supported environment are set out in your service agreement.

Questions before you start

Can you help a business that already uses Macs?

Yes. We can review your current Mac environment, account setup and support needs before recommending changes. You do not need to replace working equipment just to start the conversation.

Does A$99 include the software?

No. A$99 per person per month plus GST covers management and support. All software subscriptions and separately scoped project work are additional.
