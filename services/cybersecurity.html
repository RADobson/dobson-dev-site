// FAQ

How much does managed security cost?

It depends on the size of your business and what you need. For most firms (10-50 employees), expect $1,500-$5,000/month — a fraction of the cost of a full-time security analyst. We'll give you a clear quote after the free briefing.

We're a small business — aren't we too small to be targeted?

Unfortunately, no. Small businesses are the primary target because they typically have weaker defences. 43% of cyber attacks target small businesses, and the average cost of a breach for an Australian SMB is over $255,000.

We already have antivirus — isn't that enough?

Antivirus only catches known threats. Modern ransomware and BEC attacks bypass traditional antivirus entirely. You need detection and response — someone watching for the things antivirus misses.

What's the Essential Eight and do we need it?

The Essential Eight is the Australian Government's cybersecurity framework. If you tender for government work or work with government contractors, you'll increasingly need to demonstrate compliance. We help you get there.

How quickly can you respond to an incident?

Our MDR service provides detection within minutes and response within the hour. For active incidents, we begin containment immediately upon detection — 24/7, including weekends and public holidays.

Do we need to change our existing IT setup?

Usually not. We work with your existing infrastructure — Microsoft 365, Windows, Mac, whatever you're running. We layer security on top without disrupting how your team works.
