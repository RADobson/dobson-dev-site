// FAQ

What exactly is the “agentic AI” here?

It is not vague workflow automation. It is a set of named cybersecurity bot squads: four bots in the Essential 8 Squad, four bots in the SOC Ops Squad, and a future-state Purple Team Squad.

Who is this for?

Founder-led financial, accounting, legal, medical, and allied health firms that handle sensitive data and want a more modern cybersecurity operating model.

Who is this not for?

Firms that want generic MSSP messaging, broad AI automation consulting, or enterprise-safe sameness. This offer is intentionally narrow and specific.

Why not just sound like a normal MSSP?

Because normal MSSP messaging is saturated and forgettable. The wedge here is specific: agentic AI for cybersecurity, delivered through specialised squads with clear roles.

Do I buy all squads at once?

Not necessarily. Some firms start with the Essential 8 Squad, others with the SOC Ops Squad. The right entry point depends on your current maturity, exposure, and priorities.

Is Purple Team available now?

Not yet. Purple Team is the future-state squad focused on validation, adversary simulation, and control testing. It is part of the roadmap, but not something we present as shipping today.

Do you use AU spelling and Australian security framing?

Yes. The site, reporting, and positioning are written for an Australian audience, including Essential Eight language and AU/UK spelling conventions.
