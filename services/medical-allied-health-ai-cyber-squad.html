// WHY THIS NICHE FITS

Medical and allied health firms carry a double burden: sensitive patient information and operational consequences when systems fail. A cyber incident is not just a security problem — it can disrupt care, scheduling, billing, and trust.

// THE PAIN

Patient data sensitivity

Health information carries intense privacy expectations and a much higher emotional consequence if mishandled.

Continuity of care risk

Downtime affects more than revenue. It affects appointments, access to records, and the ability to run the practice smoothly.

Lean operations

Founder-led practices rarely have internal security leadership, but still need serious cyber capability.

Compliance pressure without clarity

Many practices know security matters, but struggle to turn that into a concrete programme with real outputs.

🚨 SOC Ops Squad

// EXAMPLE USE CASES

  • Reduce exposure to mailbox compromise, phishing, and identity abuse across clinical and admin users
  • Strengthen endpoint and application hardening in environments that cannot tolerate sloppy change
  • Improve restore confidence so backups are not just assumed to work
  • Get faster triage for suspicious events before they turn into practice-wide disruption
  • Build a more credible cybersecurity story for patients, partners, and stakeholders
