// FAQ

What's the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?

A chatbot answers questions. An agent takes action. Our agents can read your emails, draft responses, book meetings, send invoices, and update your systems — autonomously, without you typing a prompt.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We follow strict data handling practices. Your data stays within your existing systems (email provider, calendar, accounting software). We don't store your business data on our servers, and all integrations use encrypted connections.

What if the agent makes a mistake?

We build in guardrails. For the first few weeks, agents work in "supervised mode" — they draft actions and wait for your approval before executing. As you build trust, you can gradually increase their autonomy.

What tools/software do I need?

We work with whatever you're already using — Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, Xero, MYOB, most CRMs, and hundreds of other tools. No need to change your existing setup.

How long before I see results?

Our pilot runs 2 weeks. Most clients see measurable time savings within the first week. The free discovery call will give you a realistic estimate for your specific workflows.

What kind of businesses is this for?

Any small business (1-20 employees) drowning in repetitive admin. We work particularly well with accounting firms, healthcare practices, professional services, trades, and hospitality businesses on the Sunshine Coast and across regional QLD.
