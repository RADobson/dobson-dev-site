The Challenge

A growing construction firm with 50+ active subcontractors was drowning in compliance paperwork. Their office admin spent 25+ hours per week chasing White Cards, insurance certificates, Safe Work Method Statements, and induction records. Documents expired without warning. Audits were stressful scrambles.

What We Built

  • Document Tracking Agent — Monitors expiry dates across all subcontractors, sends automated reminders at 30/14/7 days before expiry
  • Email Processing Agent — Reads incoming emails with attachments, extracts document type and expiry dates, files them in the correct folder structure
  • Escalation Agent — When a subcontractor doesn't respond after 3 reminders, flags them to the site manager and blocks them from the next week's schedule

The Result

Compliance document collection went from ~70% (with constant manual chasing) to 98% within the first month. The office admin was reassigned to higher-value work. Audit preparation dropped from 2 days to 20 minutes.
