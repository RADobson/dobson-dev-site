// WHY LEGAL FIRMS FIT

Legal firms run on confidentiality, responsiveness, and trust. Email compromise, document exposure, account takeover, or operational downtime are not abstract IT issues — they are direct threats to client confidence, matter integrity, and firm reputation.

// THE PAIN

Confidential matters

Privileged communications, contracts, litigation documents, and settlement details create a high-consequence breach surface.

Email is mission-critical

Phishing, BEC, and account compromise hit especially hard when client instructions and money movement flow through inboxes.

Too small for a full cyber team

Most founder-led practices cannot justify building Essential 8 capability and SOC operations internally.

Trust is the business model

A security incident does not just create cleanup work — it damages the perception that the firm is safe to trust.
